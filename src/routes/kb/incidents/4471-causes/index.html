Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October with these two outcomes.

1. Parser flips day and month (379)

The shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants see their dates flipped, for example 4 July going out as 7 April. This covers 11 tenants and every channel.

Five of these reports read as a one-day move (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620). The desk files them under their cause, so they stay in this count.

2. The booking-v3 hint is wrong (33)

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it their way. All 33 reports are form reports from three tenants: alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour.
